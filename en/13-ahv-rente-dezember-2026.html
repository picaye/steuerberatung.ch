The 13th AHV Pension: First Payment in December 2026 – Amount, Process and Tax

What the 13th AHV pension is

On 3 March 2024 Swiss voters approved the initiative for a 13th AHV (OASI) pension. From this year, the AHV old-age pension is paid 13 times a year: the extra payment arrives in December 2026, together with the regular December pension.

You do not need to do anything and nothing has to be applied for. The compensation fund that pays your December pension calculates the 13th pension by law and transfers it automatically.

Practical tip: Ignore offers to «file the application» for a fee – no application exists. Reliable information comes from your cantonal compensation fund or the Federal Social Insurance Office at bsv.admin.ch.

How much the 13th pension is

The 13th pension equals one twelfth of the old-age pensions paid to you during the calendar year, rounded to whole francs. Anyone who received twelve full monthly pensions in 2026 therefore gets exactly one monthly pension extra.

The scale is known: with a full contribution record, the monthly AHV pension in 2026 ranges between CHF 1'260 and CHF 2'520, so a full-year recipient gets between CHF 1'260 and CHF 2'520 on top. Children's pensions, supplementary pensions and the AHV 21 transitional supplement for women are not included.

If your pension started mid-year or its amount changed, you receive one twelfth of the sum actually paid out – which is why the exact figure can only be calculated in December.

Practical tip: Estimate the amount yourself: add up your January-to-December old-age pensions and divide by twelve. You will know before Christmas what to expect – and can check the figure on your bank statement.

Who gets it – and who does not

Entitled are all persons who receive an AHV old-age pension in the month of December. And only old-age pensions are paid 13 times: IV (disability) pensions, survivors' pensions (widow's, widower's and orphan's) and children's pensions remain at twelve payments a year.

Good news for recipients of supplementary benefits: the 13th pension is expressly not counted as income in the supplementary-benefit calculation – it may neither reduce nor cancel those benefits.

Taxes: the 13th pension is regular income

Fiscally, the 13th pension is no special case: AHV old-age pensions are taxable at 100 % (Art. 22 para. 1 of the Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation, FATA) at federal, cantonal and communal level. The December payment is simply added to your annual income – no special rate, no allowance.

Arithmetically, your taxable AHV income rises by about 8.3 %. How much extra tax that costs depends on your canton, commune and remaining income: anyone below the basic allowance pays nothing; middle incomes are taxed at roughly the cantonal marginal rate.

Practical tip: Factor the extra pension into your provisional tax bills – many communes adjust interim payments at quarter-end. That way you avoid interest on late payment, which would erase the gift-like character of the December cheque.

Financing: what the vote of 29 November 2026 changes

The financing is still open: in June 2026 parliament opted for partial funding via VAT – the standard rate to rise from 8.1 to 8.5 % and the special accommodation rate from 3.8 to 4 %; the reduced rate for everyday goods stays at 2.6 %. Voters decide on 29 November 2026; the increase would take effect no earlier than 2028.

The result has no immediate impact on your payment: the modalities of the 13th pension are approved and it will be paid in December 2026 in any case. Its long-term financing, however, stays on the agenda – the AHV 2030 reform is in consultation.

In short

  1. First payment: December 2026, automatically with the December pension – no application.
  2. Amount: one twelfth of the old-age pensions paid in the calendar year, rounded to francs; with a full contribution record roughly CHF 1'260–2'520.
  3. Only old-age pensions are paid 13 times; supplementary benefits are unaffected.
  4. The 13th pension is fully taxable income, like every other monthly pension (Art. 22 para. 1 FATA).
