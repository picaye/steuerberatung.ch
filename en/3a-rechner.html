Is the maximum amount worth it?

At a 30% marginal rate you save roughly CHF 2,180 per year on a CHF 7,258 contribution – over 30 years of compounding this is Switzerland's most effective savings lever.

What is the marginal tax rate?

The percentage by which your total tax rises if your income grows by CHF 100. The calculator derives it for your municipality and income – typically 25% to 45%.

Can I catch up?

Yes: since 2026, missing contribution years from 2025 onwards can be filled retroactively (up to 10 years) on top of the annual amount.
