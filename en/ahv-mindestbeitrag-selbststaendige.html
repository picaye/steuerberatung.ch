AHV 2026: minimum contribution CHF 530 and the contribution scale for self-employed persons

What self-employed persons pay

Self-employed people pay the contributions to the AHV (Old-Age and Survivors Insurance), the DI (Disability Insurance) and the EO income replacement scheme entirely themselves – there is no employer covering half. The 2026 rate: AHV 8.1 % + DI 1.4 % + EO 0.5 % = 10.0 % in total of the net income from your self-employed activity. For context: employees and employers together share 10.6 %, 5.3 % each.

The sliding scale: lower income, lower rate

For annual income below CHF 60'500 a lower contribution rate applies – the “sliding scale” (information sheet AHV 2.02). It starts at 5.371 percent for income from CHF 10'100 and rises in steps to the full 10.0 percent from CHF 60'500. Example: with income of CHF 40'500 you pay 6.728 % ≈ CHF 2'725 instead of the full CHF 4'050. What counts is the net income that your compensation fund establishes by assessment decision.

Minimum contribution of CHF 530

If you earn less than CHF 10'100 a year, you pay the minimum contribution of CHF 530 per year (which corresponds to an annual salary of CHF 5'000). Non-working persons also pay at least CHF 530 and at most CHF 26'500. If you have already paid the minimum contribution on a salary, you can request that your self-employment contributions be charged only at the lowest scale rate (5.371 %).

Side business: if you carry out the self-employed activity only on a side basis and the annual income from it is at most CHF 2'500, contributions are charged only on your explicit request (information sheet 2.02).

AHV pensions in 2026

The full pension (with a complete contribution period) ranges in 2026 between CHF 1'260 and CHF 2'520 per month – that is CHF 15'120 to CHF 30'240 per year (BSV). Married couples receive two individual pensions, capped together at 150 percent of the maximum pension: at most CHF 3'780 per month (CHF 45'360 per year). The amount depends on your contribution period and your average income from gainful activity.

The 13th AHV pension is coming – first paid in December 2026

The voters approved the 13th old-age pension. It will be paid out for the first time together with the December 2026 pension as a supplement: one twelfth (8.333 percent) of all monthly pensions received from January to December 2026. Anyone drawing an old-age pension in December 2026 receives the supplement automatically from the AHV compensation fund – no application is required.

Common mistakes

  • Confusing the contribution base: what counts is the net profit of the self-employed activity as assessed, not a “salary”.
  • 5 % default interest: if you pay the annual bill more than 30 days after the invoice date, it gets expensive.
  • Minimum contribution paid twice despite being covered on your salary – correctable with proof.
  • Forgetting the family compensation fund: depending on the canton, a contribution to family allowances is added on top of the 10 %.

In short

  1. Self-employed: 10.0 % AHV/DI/EO (8.1 + 1.4 + 0.5), full rate from CHF 60'500.
  2. Below that: sliding scale from 5.371 % (from CHF 10'100); below CHF 10'100: minimum contribution CHF 530.
  3. Pensions 2026: CHF 1'260–2'520 per month (15'120–30'240 per year), married-couple cap CHF 3'780 per month.
  4. 13th AHV pension: first supplement with the December 2026 pension, paid automatically.
