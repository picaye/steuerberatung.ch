Privacy Policy

Controller

steuerberatung.ch, email: info@steuerberatung.ch

Principles

We process personal data exclusively within the framework of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/DSG) and, where applicable, the GDPR. Data is retained only for as long as required for the respective purpose.

Contact form

When you send us a request via the contact form, we process the data you provide (name, email address, canton, situation, message) exclusively to handle your request and for any follow-up questions. The legal basis is contract performance or your consent. The data is not passed on to third parties, except to the tax partners you have selected, and only to the extent required for the mediation.

Advertising partner requests

When you send us a request via the advertising form, we process the data you provide (company/name, email address, canton/region, interest, message) exclusively to handle your request. The legal basis is your consent. The data is not passed on to third parties.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses no third-party tracking cookies and no external analytics services (no Google Analytics, no third-party Matomo). We use our own, minimal analytics method: when a page is viewed, an anonymous page identifier, the referrer and a random session ID stored purely locally in the browser are transmitted to our own server in order to count the number of visits per page. No profiles are created, no personal data (name, email) is stored and no data is passed on to third parties; the IP address is not evaluated. The session ID can be deleted at any time by deleting this website's cookies in your browser.

Your rights

You have the right to information about the data stored about you, to rectification of incorrect data, to erasure and to restriction of processing. To exercise these rights, please contact info@steuerberatung.ch.

Changes

We reserve the right to adapt this privacy policy to changed legal requirements or to changes in our service.
