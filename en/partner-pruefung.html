How we vet our partners

“Vetted” is not a marketing word here. Every specialist we refer meets these criteria and is checked on an ongoing basis.

Mandatory criteria

  • Recognised qualification: Federal certificate of proficiency (tax expert, fiduciary) or equivalent; membership of Treuhand Suisse or EXPERTsuisse preferred.
  • Professional indemnity insurance: valid financial-loss liability cover with adequate sum insured.
  • Swiss seat and data handling: business address in the canton of referral; client data processed in Switzerland.
  • Practice with individuals: demonstrable experience with private tax returns in the relevant canton.
  • Independence: no commission-driven product sales in the portal's name; rankings cannot be bought.

The vetting process

  1. Application or enquiry via the contact form.
  2. Verification of documents: diploma, insurance, commercial register entry (SHAB number).
  3. First referrals to selected mandates with follow-up feedback.
  4. Ongoing checks: complaints or missing evidence suspend or end the partnership.

Transparency

Paid placements (sponsored posts, banners) are labelled as advertising on werbung.html and have no influence on partner vetting. A public partner directory is being built; until then, we disclose the qualification of each referred specialist at the time of referral.
