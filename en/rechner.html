Swiss Tax Calculator 2026

Federal, cantonal, municipal, church and wealth tax per the official tariffs — for every municipality. Free, no registration.

Enter the figure from your tax bill (after deductions). Example: CHF 100,000 gross for a single person gives roughly CHF 82,000 taxable.

Calculated with the official tariffs and tax multipliers of the Federal Tax Administration (Swiss Tax Calculator, simple version): every municipality, incl. church and wealth tax, with cantonal splitting procedures and child deductions. The input is taxable income; apply your personal deductions beforehand. The tax authority assessment remains binding.

Data source: ESTV swisstaxcalculator.estv.admin.ch (2026/2025 tariffs, multipliers and deduction rules). If the live connection fails, the calculator falls back to an offline canton-average estimate — the display marks the mode.
